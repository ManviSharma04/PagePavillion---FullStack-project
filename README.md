# 📚 Page Pavilion

Page Pavilion is a **comprehensive online platform for book lovers** designed to transform the online book-shopping experience.  
It offers a **genre-based bookstore**, **wishlist management**, **blogs & articles**, and **user profiles** — making it more than just a marketplace.  
It is a **virtual haven for readers** to explore, connect, and celebrate literature in the digital age.

## ✨ Features & Benefits  

✅ **Genre-based Bookstore** – Browse a wide collection of books across mystery, romance, poetry, fantasy & more.  
✅ **Wishlist** – Save and manage your favorite books.  
✅ **Add to Cart & Purchase Flow** – Smooth shopping experience.  
✅ **Blogs & Articles** – Get reviews, author insights, and literary trends.  
✅ **User Profiles** – Personalized book discovery journey.  
✅ **Search Functionality** – Quickly find books you love.  
✅ **Responsive Design** – Works seamlessly on desktop & mobile.
